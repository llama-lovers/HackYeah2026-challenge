## Phase Goal

**As a** blind user on inpost.pl, **I want to** press a shortcut, say a Polish command and have the agent click or fill what I described, **so that** I hear through my own screen reader what the agent did and what actually changed, while my sensitive data stays masked and the API key never leaves the proxy.

This plan's slice (the Walking Skeleton tracer): a real press of the shortcut on the tracking page records the user, transcribes through the proxy, snapshots and masks the page, asks the model, validates the proposal against the live element, announces "Klikam Znajdź." through the live region and clicks. After this plan a user can actually drive the page by voice; plan 01-04 adds the spoken effect read-back.
