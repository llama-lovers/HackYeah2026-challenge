## Phase Goal

**As a** blind user, **I want to** check an InPost parcel's status by voice from start to finish, **so that** the agent never takes an irreversible, secret-entering or ambiguous step without asking me first.

(Mechanical restatement of the ROADMAP Phase 2 goal line, which is not written in user-story form; no story was invented. Run `/gsd mvp-phase 2` if a curated story is wanted.)

This plan's slice: the demo scenario. "Sprawdź status przesyłki numer …" becomes a local, model-free dialog: parse the spoken number, read it back in digit groups, wait for "tak", fill and search, and quote the status from the page. It also makes numbers in the agent's speech natural Polish (OUT-05) and caps the steps of every command (ACT-05).

## Assumptions adopted (no CONTEXT.md; orchestrator-recommended resolutions of RESEARCH open questions)

- Q2: the number is read back and confirmed **before** anything is filled; fill and click then run as one bounded command.
- Q4: a not-found or site error is spoken as `Strona informuje: …`; a found status keeps `Status na stronie: …`.
- Q5: the offscreen recording cap rises from 15 s to `RECORDING_CAP_MS = 25000` (must stay below `STALE_MS = 30000` in `turn.ts`, otherwise a second key press at 30 s would start a new turn instead of stopping), plus the two-step "Podaj numer przesyłki." dialog.
- Phase 1 decisions stay binding: D-04/D-23 (spoken names from the DOM, placeholder first), D-05 (navigation handoff), D-09 (one command at a time), D-19 (proxy owns prompt/schema; this plan does not touch the proxy), D-20 (no body logging).

<objective>
Deliver "sprawdź status przesyłki numer …" end to end on the tracking page: local intent, Polish words to digits, digit-group readback, a session-stored pending confirmation answered by a locally matched "tak", fill + Znajdź through the existing validated executor, and a verbatim status read from the page DOM. Then harden the dialog (spoken-word numbers, wrong length, two-step, cancel, expiry, not-found, 25 s cap) and finish with natural Polish numbers in model sentences, a per-command step budget and a privacy-safe navigation handoff.
