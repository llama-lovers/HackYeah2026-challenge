## Phase Goal

**As a** blind user, **I want to** check an InPost parcel's status by voice from start to finish, **so that** the agent never takes an irreversible, secret-entering or ambiguous step without asking me first.

(Mechanical restatement of the ROADMAP Phase 2 goal line; no story was invented.)

This plan's slice: the agent asks instead of guessing, and it refuses to enter secrets or fight captchas. Ambiguity, whether the model notices it (new strict `choose` action) or the extension does (two controls with the same name), ends in at most three numbered options read from the page. Requests to type passwords or codes, or to solve a captcha, stop before anything leaves the browser.

## Assumptions adopted

- No CONTEXT.md. Proxy owns prompt and schema (D-19); the schema stays flat and strict (Anthropic structured outputs: no maxItems, every field required), hence option_1..option_3 rather than an array (RESEARCH "Ambiguity and numbered choices").
- The deterministic fake upstream keeps needs_confirmation false, so the tests prove that the extension, not the model, enforces confirmation and refusals.
- Phase 3 items (scroll, "powtórz", verbosity) and Phase 4 items (stop, earcons) are not touched.
